Data in agriculture comes from multiple sources such as soil sensors, weather stations, drones, and satellite imagery. These tools collect real-time information about soil moisture, nutrient levels, crop health, and climate conditions. Instead of relying on guesswork, farmers can use this data to understand exactly what their crops need and when.
